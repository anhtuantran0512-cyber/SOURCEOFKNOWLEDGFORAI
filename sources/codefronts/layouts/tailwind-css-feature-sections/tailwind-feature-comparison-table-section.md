A real, accessible <table> comparing your product against two alternatives and the spreadsheet, grouped into sections. Your column is tinted and crowned; the feature column stays sticky while the table scrolls horizontally on phones; and a row-hover highlight uses has-[td:hover]: nowhere but plain hover: on the row.
